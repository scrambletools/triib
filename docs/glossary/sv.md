## Choices a native speaker should check

- **stream / streamar** rather than ström / strömmar, and the plural
  streamar rather than streams.
- **listeners** as the plural (only in the fan-out line), rather than a
  nativized listenrar.
- **reglage** for control.
- **reservering** rather than reservation.
- **låst / låsning förlorad** for locked and lost lock.
