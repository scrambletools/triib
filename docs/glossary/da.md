## Choices a native speaker should check

- **clock** loanword (medieclock, clockkilde, clockdomæne, hardwareclock)
  rather than ur (medieur, urkilde).
- **ramme / rammer** for frames rather than the loan frame.
- **klynge** for cluster rather than the loan cluster.
- **interface** rather than grænseflade.
- **et preset** (neuter) and the plural switche.
- **låst / mistet lås** for locked and lost lock.
