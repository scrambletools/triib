Standards and profiles

ControlIEEE 1722.1-2021 ATDECC: discovery (ADP), the entity model (AECP AEM) and connections (ACMP)
MilanMilan 1.3: its connection management, GET_MILAN_INFO and the Milan view of stream state
StreamsIEEE 1722 AVTP: AAF and AM824 audio, CRF media clock streams, and MAAP for stream addresses
ReservationsIEEE 802.1Q MRP, MSRP and MVRP
TimeIEEE 802.1AS gPTP, through linuxptp's ptp4l, and each entity's gPTP path and grandmaster
AVB LiteThe AVB Lite community profile: falling back over switches that are not AVB bridges, CVU SRP declarations, its PTP profile, status and alarms

Milan and AVB are trademarks of Avnu Alliance. triib is not certified by Avnu Alliance.

Controller

DiscoveryEvery entity on the interface, with its entity model read and kept for next time, and offline entities shown as such
ConnectionsA matrix of talker outputs and listener inputs for audio and media clock streams, with each connection's state and whether the formats meet; connections by group or one by one
NetworkA map of bridges and devices from each entity's gPTP path, with audio streams, CRF streams or the clock tree drawn on it
ChangesNames and groups, stream formats, sampling rates, clock sources, channel maps, controls such as gain and mute, and identifying
DiagnosticsHow each clock domain and stream has fared, from the entity's counters, with what went wrong marked; each bound input's reservation; each stream's maximum transit time; and the descriptors themselves
PresetsClock sources, sampling rates, stream formats, controls and connections, and this computer's own talkers and listeners; recalling one changes only what differs
LogEvery ATDECC frame sent and heard, decoded, with the frames that break the rules marked
Command linetriib-cli: discover, describe, connect and disconnect, name, formats, rates, clocks, channel maps, controls, identify, the network and the stream state

Talkers and listeners of the computer's own

SystemsLinux, on a wired interface with a PTP hardware clock (ethtool -T shows one)
Timeptp4l running gPTP on that interface, read through its read-only socket
Each endpointA Milan entity with one stream, which any controller binds
FormatsAAF or AM824 at 48, 96 or 192 kHz, 8 channels unless another count is picked
AudioTo and from the computer's audio devices, one per endpoint; a talker can send a tone or silence instead, and a listener can discard what it receives
SchedulingReal-time threads through RealtimeKit, as PipeWire asks for
Programtriib-endpointd, which reads endpoints.toml again whenever it changes

AVB Lite

WatchingEach entity's mode and why it fell back, its PTP profile and offset, its media VLAN and what its streams take of each link, and the profile's alarms
Falling backThis computer's endpoints fall back when no AVB bridge answers on the interface, and come back to AVB when the link comes up again
StreamsUnicast to each listener, to 2 a talker unless set otherwise, and multicast beyond that only where allowed
ptp4lMoved between gPTP and the AVB Lite PTP profile through triib's systemd units and polkit rule
LinkEnergy-Efficient Ethernet and PAUSE kept off, as the profile asks, by a systemd unit

Languages

38, following the system's language or the one picked in Settings: Arabic, Bengali, Catalan, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Czech, Danish, Dutch, English (US and UK), Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Norwegian Bokmål, Persian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Swahili, Swedish, Tamil, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Urdu and Vietnamese. Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu are laid out right to left.

Technology

LanguageRust
Interfaceiced, with scramble-ui, the look it shares with prev
TranslationsFluent
Librariesatdecc (IEEE 1722.1 with Milan), avb-mrp (MRP, MSRP and MVRP) and avb-net (raw Ethernet, interfaces and hardware timestamps): no I/O of their own outside avb-net's platform support, and no std needed, for microcontrollers

Settings and data

Settingstriib.toml, in ~/.config on Linux, %APPDATA%\triib on Windows and ~/Library/Application Support/triib on macOS
Entity modelsKept in triib's cache, so a device seen before shows in full at once
Endpointsendpoints.toml in triib's data folder, ~/.local/share/triib on Linux
